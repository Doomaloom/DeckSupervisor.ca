Deck Supervisor
Main Port Update

A smoother way to run every session.

A major collection of improvements to imports, session setup, rosters, masterlists, schematics, attendance sheets, and reports.

This release is focused on saving staff time, reducing repeated work, and making printed materials more flexible and dependable.

Simpler, more reliable roster imports
Clearer session and dashboard workflows
Flexible masterlists with live previews
Adjustable schematic previews and covers
Familiar attendance sheets for every level
Consistent roster and report-card details
Lower operating costs behind the scenes
1

Easier CSV Imports

One streamlined flow with less repetition and clearer choices.

  • Uploaded information stays available while working with a session.
  • Locations can be adjusted without uploading the same file again.
  • Included Raw Locations are selected from a dropdown built from the uploaded file.
  • Duplicate and unnecessary upload fields have been removed.
  • Combined locations, mini sessions, waitlists, and varied schedules are handled more consistently.
  • Successful imports move directly into Manage Sessions.
2

Clearer Session Management

Common session tasks are easier to find, review, and complete.

  • Creating, selecting, reviewing, and editing sessions follows a more focused layout.
  • Session dates, week labels, and mini-session details are displayed consistently.
  • Imported information stays connected when session locations change.
  • Full-time and part-time session views behave more consistently.
  • Signing out returns directly to the login page.
3

More Powerful Masterlists

More ways to organize students, with a preview of the finished document.

  • Student rows appear correctly in both the live preview and final document.
  • Age has its own centered column.
  • Font size, borders, headings, alignment, and bold styling are adjustable.
  • Column sizing is more consistent and easier to read.
  • Option groups are compact, scrollable, and independently collapsible.
  • Schematic Coverpage settings appear first and stay separate from other formatting choices.
Class & TimeKeeps students organized under their scheduled classes.
AlphabeticalCombines students into A–Z sections, sorted by first or last name.
4

Better Schematic Planning

Review and fine-tune the final layout before printing.

  • Live preview shows the schematic before printing.
  • Portrait and landscape layouts are supported.
  • Scale can be adjusted from 60% to 120%.
  • Instructor names can be highlighted for individual packets.
  • One cover can be prepared for each instructor.
  • Student totals and class capacity remain visible at a glance.
5

Improved Attendance Sheets

The familiar class-specific designs are preserved across all 23 supported lesson templates.

  • Every level retains its own skills, headings, and assessment content.
  • Front and back pages stay in their intended order.
  • Paired and odd class packets remain complete and correctly arranged.
  • Private-lesson reference content is preserved and fits correctly.
  • Day labels, line breaks, and page breaks are easier to read.
  • Level-edited classes use the correct attendance template.
  • Instructor packets can include schematic covers and blank backs.
  • Sheets are always assembled from current roster information.
Attendance opens in the familiar Chrome or Edge print dialog, where staff can print normally or choose Save as PDF.
6

Consistent Rosters and Level Changes

Updates now carry through reliably from roster review to final documents.

  • Whole-class and individual-student level edits are supported.
  • Changing a level no longer unexpectedly changes roster order.
  • Updated levels appear consistently in rosters, attendance sheets, and report cards.
  • Related level edits stay together when attendance materials are prepared.
7

Refined Reports and Printing

Documents are more consistent, while print controls are easier to understand.

  • Previews and printed documents use the same information and formatting.
  • Session reports retain their staff, lesson, safety, facility, feedback, and project sections.
  • Blank pages, rotated covers, and combined packets keep the correct size and orientation.
  • Document titles and filenames are clearer and more consistent.
  • Popup-blocked printing offers a clearer retry experience.
8

A Smoother Everyday Experience

Small refinements reduce interruptions and make everyday work feel more polished.

  • Disruptive browser alerts have been replaced with cleaner in-app feedback.
  • Print choices are grouped logically and are easier to scan.
  • Additional safeguards improve reliability across imports, sessions, rosters, attendance, and reports.
  • The service can pause while idle to lower running costs and resume automatically when needed.
The first action after a long idle period may take a moment while the service wakes up. Following actions should respond normally.

In short

This update makes Deck Supervisor easier to navigate, reduces repeated work, gives staff more control over printed materials, and keeps roster information consistent from import through final reports.
